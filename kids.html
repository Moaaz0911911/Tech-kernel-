Adventure 2

The secret light code (binary)

Deep down, computers only understand two things: ON and OFF — like a light switch! We write ON as 1 and OFF as 0. Each bulb below is worth a number. Turn bulbs on and add up their numbers to make any number you want!

00000 = 0

All the lights are off. That’s zero!

🕵️ Your name in secret code

Computers store every letter as a number. Capital A is 65, which is 01000001 in binary!

Pixel painter

Look really, really closely at a screen and you’ll find it’s made of tiny coloured squares called pixels. Every photo, game and video is just lots of pixels! Paint your own pixel picture — tap or drag on the grid.

Adventure 5

How the internet delivers messages

When you send a message, the internet chops it into tiny pieces called packets. Each packet gets a number and an address, like a little parcel. The packets can travel different roads through helpers called routers, and the computer at the end puts them back in order!

Adventure 6

Password power-up

A password is a secret key for your accounts. Short, easy passwords are like a lock made of jelly — hackers can guess them fast! Long passwords with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols are super strong. Feed the password monster and watch it grow!

    🥚

    I’m just an egg… feed me a password!

    🦸 Password rules for superheroes:
    • Use a silly sentence, like Purple-Tiger-Eats-42-Tacos!
    • Never share your password with friends — only with your parent or guardian.
    • Use a different password for every account.
