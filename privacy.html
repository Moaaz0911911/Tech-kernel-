kerNEl

Privacy & your data

Short version: no email, no phone number, no ads, no tracking. You can delete everything at any time.

What kerNEl stores

  • Your account: username and password. The password is stored only as a scrambled hash, never as the password itself. Also a hash of your recovery code.
  • Your profile: nickname, avatar or photo, bio, favourite topics, hat and frame, language.
  • Your progress: XP, streak, achievements, saved stories, reactions and game scores.
  • What you post: chat messages, comments, private messages, votes and reports.
  • People: who you follow, who you blocked, and who invited you.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, your browser's push address, your time zone and which notifications you want.
  • Safety: your IP address is used for a short time (up to an hour) to stop spam and too many sign-ups.

Who can see it

  • Everyone: your username, nickname, avatar or photo, bio, XP, rank, streak, followers, chat messages and comments. You can hide yourself from the leaderboard.
  • Only you and the other person: private messages. Moderators only see messages that someone reported (the reported person's last few messages in that chat).
  • Only you: your password, saved stories and settings.

How long it's kept

  • Chat messages: 30 days. Private messages: 1 year. Notifications: 30 days.
  • Everything else stays until you delete it or your account.

Your choices

  • Change or remove your photo, bio and nickname any time.
  • Choose who can message you, block people, and turn notifications off.
  • Delete your account in Me → Account & security. It removes your profile, progress, messages, comments, follows and photo straight away.

Other services

  • kerNEl runs on Cloudflare. News comes from public sources, and story pictures load from the news websites.
  • If AI explanations are switched on, only the story headline and your question are sent to Anthropic (Claude) to write the answer.

Kids

kerNEl Kids works without an account and keeps progress on the device. Kids who use accounts or messages should do it with a grown-up.

Datenschutz & deine Daten

Kurz gesagt: keine E-Mail, keine Telefonnummer, keine Werbung, kein Tracking. Du kannst jederzeit alles löschen.

Was kerNEl speichert

  • Dein Konto: Benutzername und Passwort. Das Passwort wird nur als verschlüsselter Hash gespeichert, nie im Klartext. Außerdem ein Hash deines Wiederherstellungscodes.
  • Dein Profil: Spitzname, Avatar oder Foto, Bio, Lieblingsthemen, Hut und Rahmen, Sprache.
  • Dein Fortschritt: XP, Streak, Erfolge, gespeicherte Storys, Reaktionen und Spielergebnisse.
  • Was du postest: Chatnachrichten, Kommentare, private Nachrichten, Abstimmungen und Meldungen.
  • Leute: wem du folgst, wen du blockiert hast und wer dich eingeladen hat.
  • Benachrichtigungen: wenn du sie einschaltest, die Push-Adresse deines Browsers, deine Zeitzone und welche Benachrichtigungen du willst.
  • Sicherheit: deine IP-Adresse wird kurz (bis zu einer Stunde) genutzt, um Spam und zu viele Anmeldungen zu stoppen.

Wer es sehen kann

  • Alle: Benutzername, Spitzname, Avatar oder Foto, Bio, XP, Rang, Streak, Follower, Chatnachrichten und Kommentare. Du kannst dich in der Bestenliste ausblenden.
  • Nur du und die andere Person: private Nachrichten. Moderatoren sehen nur Nachrichten, die jemand gemeldet hat (die letzten paar Nachrichten der gemeldeten Person in diesem Chat).
  • Nur du: dein Passwort, gespeicherte Storys und Einstellungen.

Wie lange es bleibt

  • Chatnachrichten: 30 Tage. Private Nachrichten: 1 Jahr. Benachrichtigungen: 30 Tage.
  • Alles andere bleibt, bis du es oder dein Konto löschst.

Deine Möglichkeiten

  • Foto, Bio und Spitzname jederzeit ändern oder entfernen.
  • Festlegen, wer dir schreiben darf, Leute blockieren und Benachrichtigungen ausschalten.
  • Konto löschen unter Ich → Konto & Sicherheit. Das entfernt Profil, Fortschritt, Nachrichten, Kommentare, Follows und Foto sofort.

Andere Dienste

  • kerNEl läuft auf Cloudflare. Die News kommen aus öffentlichen Quellen, Story-Bilder laden von den News-Seiten.
  • Wenn KI-Erklärungen eingeschaltet sind, werden nur die Schlagzeile und deine Frage an Anthropic (Claude) geschickt, um die Antwort zu schreiben.

Kinder

kerNEl Kids funktioniert ohne Konto und speichert den Fortschritt auf dem Gerät. Kinder, die Konten oder Nachrichten nutzen, sollten das mit einem Erwachsenen tun.

Fragen

Eröffne ein Issue auf kerNEls GitHub-Seite.

Privacy at ang data mo

Sa madaling salita: walang email, walang phone number, walang ads, walang tracking. Puwede mong burahin ang lahat anumang oras.

Ano ang ini-store ng kerNEl

  • Ang account mo: username at password. Naka-store lang ang password bilang scrambled hash, hindi kailanman ang mismong password. Pati hash ng recovery code mo.
  • Ang profile mo: nickname, avatar o litrato, bio, mga paboritong topic, sombrero at frame, wika.
  • Ang progress mo: XP, streak, achievements, mga naka-save na story, reactions at score sa mga laro.
  • Mga pino-post mo: chat messages, comments, private messages, mga boto at report.
  • Mga tao: sino ang fina-follow mo, sino ang na-block mo, at sino ang nag-invite sa’yo.
  • Notifications: kung bubuksan mo, ang push address ng browser mo, ang time zone mo at kung aling notifications ang gusto mo.
  • Kaligtasan: ginagamit sandali (hanggang isang oras) ang IP address mo para pigilan ang spam at sobrang daming sign-up.

Sino ang nakakakita

  • Lahat: username, nickname, avatar o litrato, bio, XP, rank, streak, followers, chat messages at comments. Puwede mong itago ang sarili mo sa leaderboard.
  • Ikaw at ang kausap mo lang: private messages. Nakikita lang ng moderators ang mga message na ni-report ng isang tao (ang huling ilang message ng ni-report sa chat na iyon).
  • Ikaw lang: ang password mo, naka-save na story at settings.

Gaano katagal ito itinatago

  • Chat messages: 30 araw. Private messages: 1 taon. Notifications: 30 araw.
  • Nananatili ang lahat ng iba hanggang burahin mo ito o ang account mo.

Ang mga pagpipilian mo

  • Palitan o tanggalin ang litrato, bio at nickname mo anumang oras.
  • Piliin kung sino ang puwedeng mag-message sa’yo, mag-block ng tao, at patayin ang notifications.
  • Burahin ang account mo sa Ako → Account at seguridad. Agad nitong tatanggalin ang profile, progress, messages, comments, follows at litrato mo.

Ibang serbisyo

  • Tumatakbo ang kerNEl sa Cloudflare. Galing ang balita sa mga pampublikong source, at naglo-load ang mga litrato ng story mula sa mga news website.
  • Kapag naka-on ang AI explanations, ang headline lang ng story at ang tanong mo ang ipinapadala sa Anthropic (Claude) para isulat ang sagot.

Mga bata

Gumagana ang kerNEl Kids nang walang account at sa device naka-save ang progress. Ang mga batang gumagamit ng account o messages ay dapat may kasamang matanda.
